# JemTek-Flow — Permanent Rules

## Token Efficiency
Never print full files, tables, or long summaries in chat. Reply in 2-3 lines max after committing.

## Visual Style: "JemTek Neon Wireframe"
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920 vertical, 60fps
- **Background**: Pitch-black (#000000)
- **Floor**: Dashed line at Y=1450 with mirrored/blurred reflection (`scaleY(-1)`, `opacity: 0.18`, `blur(4px)`)
- **Atmosphere**: Floating dust particles
- **Glow**: SVG `<feGaussianBlur>` neon bloom filter on all vectors/text
